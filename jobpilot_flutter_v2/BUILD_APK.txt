JOBPILOT APK BUILD
1. Upload this Flutter folder's contents to a private GitHub repository.
2. Open Actions.
3. Select "Build JobPilot Android APK".
4. Click "Run workflow".
5. Download the JobPilot-Android-APK artifact from the completed run.
6. Extract app-release.apk and install it on Android.

No Android Studio or Flutter installation is required on your PC.
The app asks for your hosted HTTPS backend URL on the login screen.
